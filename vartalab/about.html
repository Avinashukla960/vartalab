About VARTALAB

"Varta" means conversation. VARTALAB is a student-focused English practice platform built for India — where classrooms teach rules but rarely give you a stage. We give you both.

📘

LEARN

Grammar lessons, quizzes, videos and flashcards with instant feedback and XP. Master the rules first.

🤝

CO-LAB

Communities by goal and level — IELTS, interviews, boards, speaking. Ask doubts, share wins, practise together.

🎤

ACT-LAB

Debates, movie nights, poetry, mock interviews and competitions — real-time English, online and in Indore.

🎯 Our mission

Every student deserves to speak English with confidence — not just pass grammar tests. We believe the best way to learn is to practise in public: answer questions, speak in sessions, compete in contests, and get feedback from peers. VARTALAB turns English practice into a daily habit with visible progress, ratings and streaks.

  • ✅ Bite-sized lessons that respect your schedule
  • ✅ Realistic mock tests & IELTS-style practice
  • ✅ Weekly contests with ratings that actually move
  • ✅ Communities and events that make practice social
  • ✅ Free for students — no paywalls, ever

⚙️ How this demo works

This is a hackathon demo. Everything runs in your browser: no backend, no login, no real AI. Your progress (rating, lessons, weak areas, contest results) is saved in localStorage as "Demo Student — Aarav Sharma".

Reset by clearing site data, or just keep practising!

🛡️ Terms & Privacy

No personal data leaves your device in this demo. The full product would follow India's DPDP Act and keep student data encrypted and consent-based. Practice content here is illustrative — not official IELTS material.
